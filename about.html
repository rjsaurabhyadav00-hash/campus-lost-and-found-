About Campus Lost & Found

A smart and simple platform designed to help students report, search and recover lost items within the campus.

Who We Are

Campus Lost & Found is a digital platform created to make the process of finding lost belongings easier and faster for students and staff. Users can report lost items, report found items and search for matching items through one centralized platform.

Our Mission

Our mission is to reduce the chances of permanently losing valuable belongings on campus by providing a reliable, organized and user-friendly Lost & Found management system.

What Our System Provides

🔍

Search Items

Easily search for lost or found items using relevant information.

📢

Report Lost Items

Students can quickly report their lost belongings with item details.

🎒

Report Found Items

Found items can be reported so that their owners can identify and claim them.

How It Works

1

Report

Report your lost or found item with the required details.

2

Search & Match

Search available items and find a possible match.

3

Claim

Submit a claim request and recover your belongings.

Why Use Campus Lost & Found?

Instead of relying on notice boards, WhatsApp groups or word-of-mouth communication, our system provides a centralized platform where lost and found information can be managed efficiently. It saves time, improves communication and increases the possibility of returning lost belongings to their rightful owners.
